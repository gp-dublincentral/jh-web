Housing - Affordable, quality homes for everyone

I’ll treat the housing crisis like the emergency it is, with ambitious, credible solutions to provide safe and secure housing. I’ll work to:

  • Deliver public housing

    Investing in new cost-rental and social homes while improving standards in our existing housing stock.

  • Tackle vacancy and dereliction

    Turning empty properties into homes and forcing owners of derelict properties to develop or sell.

  • Put affordability first

    Activating vacant spaces above shops and rezoning suitable land so we can build faster and cheaper.

  • Make home-ownership achievable

    Cracking down on investors bulk-buying properties so we can free up homes for families.

  • Improve homeless services

    Delivering nationwide services with proper supports so care isn’t concentrated or stretched too thin.

Climate - Caring for people, caring for our planet

A Green TD will be the strongest advocate for the nature we love, the environment we share and the climate action we need. I’ll push for:

  • Better public transport

    Accelerating delivery of public transport services that are reliable, affordable and accessible.

  • Safe roads AND Clean Air

    Pushing for the infrastructure that makes walking and cycling a safe choice for everyone.

  • Cleaner, cheaper energy

    Getting us off expensive, imported fossil fuels and on to clean, renewable, Irish energy.

  • Warmer homes

    Ramping up retrofitting to make our homes warmer, healthier and cheaper to heat.

  • Nature restoration

    Protecting our environment so we have cleaner water, greener streets and spaces where nature can thrive

Culture - Supporting a dynamic, creative city

Our cultural and artistic spaces nurture talent, foster community and make Dublin Central the vibrant place that it is. I promise to:

  • Protect our cultural spaces

    Resisting the erosion of independent venues and creative spaces across our city.

  • Prioritise a liveable city

    Developing new civic spaces where communities can come together and culture and sport can thrive.

  • Nurture local talent

    Providing the facilities and supports for our athletes, artists and creatives to grow and develop their skills.

  • Celebrate diversity

    Strengthening integration programmes to combat division and build an inclusive, welcoming society.

  • Enhance our night life

    Reforming the laws to help make Dublin’s late-night options fun, vibrant and worth staying out for.

Care - Creating a care-centred society

Care is the backbone of our communities and carers should be recognised, respected and supported. I will champion:

  • Affordable public childcare

    Building on the work of the Green Party to further reduce childcare costs and support educators.

  • More school places

    Increasing local capacity and improving supports so children of all abilities have access to education.

  • Community models of care

    Reducing waiting lists by focusing on provision of accessible, affordable community-based healthcare.

  • Better disability supports

    Funding community-based services and investing in the accessibility of public spaces.

  • Mental health and addiction care

    Taking a health-led approach to addiction and ensuring mental health services offer 24/7 crisis support
